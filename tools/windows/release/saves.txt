IzunaRecomp - import Xbox 360 saves (optional).

Copy Xbox 360 Ninja Gaiden II save folders here (folders that hold the save
file and its header, as exported from a console or another recompilation),
then open SAVES in the hub and choose Import. Every save in this folder and
its subfolders is imported; anything that is not a save is skipped.

Your saves live in the user\ folder beside ng2.exe. Copy that folder along
when you move to a new version.
